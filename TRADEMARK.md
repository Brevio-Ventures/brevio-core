## What you may do without asking

- **Show the badge.** The static "checked by brevio-core" badge that `brevio-core init` prints may go in your README,
  linking to this repository.
- **Keep the footer.** The line "checked by brevio-core" that the Action adds to its pull request comment may stay;
  the `footer` input turns it off.
- **Name it.** Say that your project uses, works with, supports or is compared with brevio-core, in text, links and
  package descriptions, as long as the relationship is clear and you do not suggest that Brevio endorses you.

## What needs written permission

- naming a fork, product, package, company or domain in a way that suggests it is brevio-core or comes from Brevio;
- using the logo, or a confusingly similar one;
- publishing a modified version under the same name.

## Forks

A fork of the code, or of the agent skill (`skills/brevio-core`), drops the marks: rename it, and take "brevio" out of
its package name, its skill name, its title and its description. You may say that it is based on brevio-core.
