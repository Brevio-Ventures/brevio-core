# R5.5: Process & lock safety

**Short answer:** Every subprocess.run carries timeout=; locks use try/finally. A child process or lock with no limit can hang the whole job for ever.

Group: Reliability and fallbacks. Status: stable. Generated from the rule definitions; do not edit by hand.

## What it catches

Process & lock safety: every subprocess.run carries timeout=; locks use try/finally.

## Why

A child process or lock with no limit can hang the whole job for ever.

## A bad example

```py
subprocess.run(cmd)
```

## A good example

```py
subprocess.run(cmd, timeout=60)
```
